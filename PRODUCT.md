# Hampton Estates Jersey

## Register

Product for the staff panel; the public website is a brand surface. This context records the existing scope in DESIGN.md and the roadmap, not new product decisions.

## Users and purpose

The Hampton Estates team manages applicants, listings, viewings and agency work in the authenticated panel. Visitors browse Jersey properties and register a search or ask a question on the public website. Automations should remove repeated work while leaving approval and exceptions understandable to the team.

## Character and boundaries

A discreet, warm, established family agency in St Helier. Property photography leads the public site. The panel prioritizes concrete decisions, readable information and clear next actions. Preserve the existing Tenor Sans/Montserrat typography and warm neutral palette in DESIGN.md; avoid new decorative dashboards, gradients, unrelated product imitations and invented metrics.

## Accessibility and trust

English interface text, native labelled controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, useful empty/error states, mobile layouts and 200% zoom. Personal data stays in authenticated flows and out of browser storage, public artifacts and automation logs. DESIGN.md defines current tokens and separates proposed improvements.
