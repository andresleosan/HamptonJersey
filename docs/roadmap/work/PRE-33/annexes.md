## Annex A · Privacy notice

Last updated 25 November 2026.

This notice explains how Hampton Estates uses the personal details you give us, for example when you register your search or ask for a viewing, and the rights you have under the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018.

Property tours and videos load only when you choose to open them. The player's provider (Matterport, YouTube or Vimeo) then receives your IP address and may use its own storage.

### Who we are

Hampton Estates, [agency postal address]. We decide how your details are used, so we are the controller of your data.

### What we collect

- Your name, email address and mobile number, and how, when and in which language you prefer us to contact you.
- What you are looking for: renting, buying or a business transfer, areas, the kind and size of home or commercial property, intended use, budgets, timing, pets and anything else you tell us.
- Your housing status in Jersey how many adults and children would live in the home, and the children’s ages you choose to tell us.
- Whether you accepted this notice and whether you asked for our news, with the date.
- For a viewing request: the property, the day and the time you chose.
- If you use the chat on our website: your name, the email or mobile number you give, your messages and our replies, the property you were looking at and when you last had the chat open.
- If you write to our Facebook page: your messages and our replies, the times, and the identifier Facebook gives us for your account.
- If you ask us to sell, let or value your property: your name, telephone number and email address, the property's address, whether you want to sell or let it and whether you asked for a valuation.

### Why we use it

- To look for properties or businesses that match your search and tell you about them.
- To arrange viewings and keep a record of what we have discussed with you.
- To answer the messages you send us through the chat on our website or our Facebook page.
- To contact you about selling or letting your property and, if you ask for one, to arrange a valuation.
- To show on a property's page how many people are interested in it: a number only, from 2 people, never your name or details.
- To send you news about homes and the Jersey market, only if you ticked the box for it.

### Our legal basis

We use your details to take the steps you ask us to take before you rent or buy a property or acquire a business, and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping an accurate record of our dealings with you and in showing, without naming anyone, how much interest a property has. We send you news only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

### Who else handles it

We do not sell your details. Our website and records are hosted by Cloudflare, which also runs the anti-spam check on our forms. The emails our app sends and the replies to them go through Hampton Estates' Gmail account, provided by Google. Our systems are built and maintained for us by Optimyze. They all act only on our instructions. When a provider handles data outside Jersey, the law requires suitable safeguards, and we only use providers that give them. We pass your details to a landlord, a seller or their agent only when you ask us to arrange a viewing or make an offer.

Our pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page opens. Matterport, YouTube and Vimeo, which play our property tours and videos, receive it only when you open one. Each uses what it receives under its own privacy policy.

### Search statistics

When anyone uses the search filters on our website, we add one to a daily total for each choice, for example "to rent" or "2 bedrooms". These totals are not linked to you, your device or your other choices, and we delete them after 90 days.

### How long we keep it

While any of your searches is active or awaiting our team's review, we keep your contact details and search record so we can continue helping you. After 11 months without activity on your record, our team reviews whether you still need the search. A viewing, offer or period without contact does not close it automatically.

Once our team has marked all your searches as resolved, we keep your details for 12 months from the last activity on your record, including the closure, a registration, a recorded change, a viewing request, an offer or a conversation with us. An open offer also protects your record while it is being negotiated. Resolving a search does not delete it.

Viewing requests have their own retention period: they are deleted 12 months after the day of the viewing. If you make an offer that leads to an agreed sale, we keep the sale record and your details for 5 years after it completes or falls through, as anti-money-laundering record-keeping rules require.

A request to sell, let or value your property is deleted 12 months after its last activity: when you sent it or when our team last updated it.

### Web chat

A person from Hampton Estates reads and answers every message; the chat does not reply automatically. We use what you write only to answer you, in the chat or by contacting you. We delete a conversation 30 days after its last message. If you ask us to register your search or arrange a viewing, those details are kept as explained above. While the conversation is still open on your device, you can delete it yourself with "Delete chat"; at any time, you can ask us to delete it.

### Messages on Facebook

If you write to our Facebook page, your message reaches us through Facebook (Meta). In our own systems we keep the text of your messages, our replies, the times and the identifier Facebook gives us for your account; we do not save attachments. A person from Hampton Estates reads and answers every message; our app sends no automatic replies. We use what you write only to answer you, on Facebook or by contacting you. If you ask us to register your search or arrange a viewing, those details are kept as explained above. We delete a conversation from our systems 30 days after its last message, and you can ask us to delete it sooner at any time. Deleting it from our systems does not delete it from Facebook: it stays in your Messenger and in our page's inbox there.

### Email sent to the app

When you reply to an email from our app, Hampton Estates can read your reply in its staff panel. These emails are sent and received through Hampton Estates' Gmail account (Google), on our instructions, and a copy stays in that account as with any email we exchange with you. A few, such as sign-in codes and replies to our app's own reply address, go through Cloudflare. In our app, email content is removed after 30 days; delivery and receipt records are removed after 90 days. Attachments are not saved. A minimal fingerprint of a received message is kept for up to 90 days so deleting a message does not let a duplicate delivery restore it. If you ask us to stop sending to your address, we keep a fingerprint of the address to respect that preference until you ask us to remove it. You can contact us to ask for deletion or to change your sending preferences.

### Landlords and tenants of homes we manage

If we manage a home you let or rent, we keep:

- For a landlord: your name, email address and phone number, the homes we manage for you, our service and fee, the repair amount you approve in advance and the approvals you give, and your monthly statements.
- For a tenant: the lead tenant's name, email address and phone number, the names of the other occupants, the tenancy's dates, rent and notice, the payments we receive and any rent owed, the deposit and its protection, the entry and exit inventories, inspections and the repairs reported.

We use these details to run the tenancy: to manage the agreement, its renewals and notices, collect the rent, protect and settle the deposit, arrange repairs, keep the home's legal checks up to date and send the landlord a monthly statement of the rent collected, our fees and repair costs. Our legal basis is the contract: our management agreement with the landlord and the tenancy agreement. Where the law requires something, such as protecting the deposit with mydeposits or keeping our accounts, our legal basis is that legal obligation.

We keep tenancy, payment, deposit and repair records, with the details in them, while we manage the home or the tenancy lasts and for 6 years after it ends. The 12-month periods above for searches do not apply to them.

### Your rights

- Access: ask for a copy of the details we hold about you.
- Rectification: have wrong or incomplete details corrected.
- Erasure: have your details deleted.
- Objection to marketing (Article 36): tell us to stop sending you news at any time, and we stop straight away.
- Withdrawing consent: this does not affect what we did with your consent before.

You can also complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner at jerseyoic.org.

### In your browser

While you fill in Register your search, your answers about the home you want are kept in that browser tab, so a reload does not lose them. Your name, contact details and housing status are not. They are cleared when you send the form or close the tab. If you start a chat, this browser keeps a random key, not your details, so you can come back to the conversation for 7 days. "End chat" removes it.

### Contact us

To ask about your data or use any of these rights, email [agency email], write to us at the address above or call [agency phone].

## Annex F · Housing category checks

The tables record how the current software classifies an applicant’s declared status. Please mark corrections against the relevant row and column.

**Renting**

| Applicant's status | Registered home | Qualified home | Category not given |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entitled, Permanent Entitled, Licensed | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed |
| Licensed for Lease Only | Allowed | Allowed (note: "lease under 9 years") | Allowed |
| Entitled for Work Only | Allowed | Excluded | To confirm |
| Registered / Standard | Allowed | Excluded | To confirm |

**Buying**

| Applicant's status | Freehold, flying freehold or leasehold (Royal Court) | Share transfer, Registered home | Share transfer, Qualified home | Share transfer, category not given |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entitled, Permanent Entitled, Licensed | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed |
| Licensed for Lease Only | To confirm (only jointly with an eligible partner) | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed |
| Entitled for Work Only | To confirm (only jointly with an eligible partner) | Allowed | To confirm (occupies only with an eligible partner) | To confirm |
| Registered / Standard | Excluded | Allowed | Excluded | To confirm |

If a sale has no tenure, the last three statuses in the table require confirmation. For a first-time buyer restriction, a known ineligible status or a recorded need to sell first fails the check. Entitled and Permanent Entitled applicants recorded as first-time buyers pass it. Other unresolved cases go to staff. Over-55 restrictions always require confirmation. If the applicant's status is unknown, a Qualified home requires confirmation. A Registered home passes this category check for rent or share transfer. For other purchase tenures, the software leaves the housing check unrated unless another restriction applies.
