# Hampton team channel on Telegram

A short guide for the Hampton team.

## What it is

A private Telegram channel where the Hampton app posts:

- a short alert for each new applicant and each viewing request, with a link to the panel;
- the summary at 08:00 Jersey time: counts of what is waiting, the listings to confirm, and a link to the panel.

Posts carry numbers, listing references, listing titles and links to the panel. They never carry names, emails, phone
numbers or anything else about a client. To see who it is, open the link and sign in to the panel.

The channel is read-only. Only the app's bot posts there, and nobody can give the bot orders from the channel. There is
no chat or discussion group.

## Joining is voluntary

You do not have to join. Everything in the channel is also in the panel (Inbox and the bar's counts). If you join, you
can mute it or leave at any time.

To join, ask for the invite link. Opening it sends a request; the channel owner (today Luis) approves each one. The link
is never shared outside the team or posted anywhere public.

## Never write anything about clients

- Do not forward channel posts outside the team.
- Do not write, forward or screenshot client details into Telegram: no names, emails, phone numbers, addresses,
  budgets, documents or messages from clients. Telegram is not covered by our data-processing agreements.
- Work on a client always happens in the panel.

## Removing someone

When someone leaves the team or should no longer see the channel, the channel owner:

1. Removes them from the channel (Telegram: channel info, Subscribers, remove).
2. Revokes the invite link and creates a new one, so the old link stops working.
3. Removes their panel access in **Settings → Users → Remove access**.

## If something looks wrong

If a post shows anything about a person, or the 08:00 summary does not arrive, tell the channel owner. Failed posts
already send the owner a private notice.
